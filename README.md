# ShredCap - Plan Your Mountain Adventure

ShredCap is a web application designed to help skiers and snowboarders easily plan their trips to Mt. Hood, Oregon. The name "ShredCap" combines "shred" (a slang term for skiing/snowboarding) and "recap" (implying a summary of information), with "Cap" also referencing the mountain's summit.

Our mission is to empower ski resort visitors with the information they need to plan their next trip with ease, starting with a focus on Timberline at Mt. Hood.

## Features (Planned)

*   **Homepage:** A welcoming entry point with stunning visuals of Mt. Hood and easy navigation.
*   **Forecast Page:** Real-time and next-day weather forecasts (temperature, precipitation) for Timberline Lodge.
*   **Historical Data:** Interactive graphs showing historical temperature and snow depth data for the past three years.
*   **About Page:** Information about ShredCap's mission and goals.
