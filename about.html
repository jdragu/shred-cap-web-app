The Mission.

ShredCap was born out of a team of local Portlanders looking for an info source for all things Mt. Hood. We're a team of data scientists by day, shredders by sick days off from, that can tell you the likelihood of Magic Mile or Palmer being open based on wind gust forecasts and historical data.

We got the data and the nerd knowledge. Catch you on the next bluebird day up by Silcox Hut.
